About Me

I am a researcher and AI engineer building intelligent robotic systems that perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world. Specifically, I work on embodied and physical AI: training foundation models that combine general multimodal understanding with reliable real-world action.

Currently, I work at X Square Robot, where I lead a critical part of our foundation model research and have been a core contributor to:

  • Wall-OSS-0.5 — our open-source 4B vision-language-action foundation model, whose pretrained checkpoint already completes real-robot manipulation tasks before any task-specific fine-tuning;
  • WALL-B logistics sorting — fully autonomous sorting of real-world parcels, deployed in live production;
  • TwinDEX — an embodiment-consistent, high-efficiency data collection system for dexterous hands.

I have also contributed to X-Tokenizer and WALL-WM.

Previously, I was a researcher at Microsoft Research Asia in Li Zhao’s group, where I worked on reinforcement learning and embodied foundation models (latent action models, VLA, visual navigation).

More broadly, I hope my research benefits the vast majority of people, making what physical intelligence can offer accessible to everyone.

Join Us

I hope to work with exceptional people who share the vision of physical AGI, as interns or full-time colleagues. I especially value:

  • Frontier AI fluency — deep command of frontier LLMs and agents, and the engineering to build agent-native pipelines that automate physical-AI research and data workflows;
  • Embodied data expertise — mastery of the full embodied data stack, from collection and quality control to training, built into rigorous, reproducible systems;
  • Research depth — genuine depth in a chosen area, and the judgment to identify and rigorously test the questions that matter on the path to physical AGI;
  • Strong foundations — solid mathematics and excellent engineering skills.

If you are interested, or would like to exchange ideas, feel free to reach out.

Featured Work

Wall-OSS-0.5

An open-source 4B vision-language-action foundation model, pretrained across 20+ robot embodiments, that already completes real-robot manipulation tasks before any task-specific fine-tuning.

Paper Code Model Project

WALL-B Logistics Sorting

Our WALL-B embodied foundation model drives two robot arms to sort highly variable real-world parcels fully autonomously: 1,816 parcels per hour at over 98% accuracy in a public livestream, and now deployed on a live production line with a leading logistics company.

News Xinhua

TwinDEX

An embodiment-consistent, high-efficiency data collection system for dexterous hands: a wearable device and a matching robot hand share one design, so human demonstrations transfer directly to the robot at scale.

Project

villa-X

A visual-language-latent-action model that plans in imagination: from an image and an instruction, it predicts latent actions and the motion they lead to, bridging high-level planning and low-level robot execution.

Paper Code Model Project

X-Tokenizer

A multimodal action tokenizer that serves as a semantic interface between vision-language reasoning and continuous robot control.

WALL-WM

A world action model that learns from semantic action events rather than fixed-length action chunks.
